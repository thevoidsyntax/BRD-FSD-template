## 1. Background

Two to three paragraphs. What is happening now, and why it is a problem.

Write this for someone who has never seen the process. If a sponsor cannot understand the problem from this section alone, the rest of the document will not save it.

## 2. Problem Statement

One paragraph. State the problem, not the solution.

Bad: "We need a workflow system."
Good: "Approval requests take an average of four days, and 60% of that is time spent locating where a request is sitting."
